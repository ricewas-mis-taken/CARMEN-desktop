Carmen: twenty openings

Two title sequences for Carmen Focus. Each starts from an idea taken from focus itself (a lens, a shadow, sand, a pendulum, a countdown) and keeps to one palette. Click one to watch it, replay it, or drag the line at the bottom to scrub through it.

What the research said. Saul Bass treated the title as part of the film itself, using flat cut-paper shapes and type that moves with them. Kyle Cooper's Se7en made the lettering the story: scratched, jittering, cut hard to a beat. Both are about restraint: one idea per sequence, a clear start and a clear end, and type that earns its place. Every opening below takes one idea and commits to it.
